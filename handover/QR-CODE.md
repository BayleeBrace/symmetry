# Booking QR code

`qr-book.svg` and `qr-book.png` point at https://symmetrywales.com/book.

- Print the SVG for anything larger than a business card; it stays sharp at any size.
- Keep the code at least 40 mm wide, black on white or bone, with clear space around it the width of one of its small squares.
- Put it on the price poster, the A-board and the counter, with "Book online" beside it and nothing else.
- Test it with a phone camera before it goes to print.
