# Symmetry photo brief

Five frames and three portraits. Phone camera is fine if the light is right.

## Setup
- Mirror lights on, room lights off. Shoot in the evening or with the blinds down so the mirrors do the lighting.
- Wipe the mirrors and clear the counter of bottles, cables and phones. One pair of clippers and a comb is enough.
- Hold the phone at chest height and keep it level. No flash, no filters.

## The five frames
1. **The chair.** Empty chair square to the lit mirror, from about two metres back. This is the website hero, so leave space on the left of the frame for the headline.
2. **The counter.** Clippers, scissors and a comb on the black counter, shot from above at a slight angle.
3. **The door.** The shared entrance on Brewery Terrace from the pavement, so people recognise it.
4. **A cut in progress.** Over the shoulder, hands and clippers in focus, the customer's face not needed.
5. **The three of you.** Sean, Travis and Dylan together in front of the mirror wall, relaxed, not posed.

## Portraits
- One each of Sean, Travis and Dylan. Same wall, same distance, same height, straight on, looking at the camera.
- Portrait orientation, head and shoulders with room above the head. They will be cropped to 3:4 and shown in black and white so the three match.

## Send
- Original files, not screenshots or WhatsApp copies (WhatsApp compresses them). AirDrop, email or a shared album.
- Two lines about each barber to go with the portrait: how long they have cut hair, what they like doing, one human thing.
